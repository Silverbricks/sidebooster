# About SellEasy

SellEasy is a **verified Australian online marketplace with approved prize promotions**.

- **Buyers** browse and buy products, send enquiries, keep a wishlist, and claim or buy tickets for prize draws, all from one account.
- **Sellers** are identity-checked and approved before they can sell. Each listing is also reviewed before it goes live. Approved sellers can, with admin permission, run prize promotions linked to their products.
- **The platform (Super Admin)** controls every approval, collects commission on approved sales, runs auditable draws and publishes verifiable results.

## Core principles

| Rule | What it means in the system |
|---|---|
| No verification = no selling | Sellers must verify email and mobile, upload documents, be approved by an admin and have a verified payout account before they can list. |
| No verified payment = no order | Orders and tickets are only confirmed by a **signed webhook** from the payment provider, never by the buyer's browser. |
| No approved sale = no commission | Commission is created only after a sale is approved. Refunds and cancellations reverse it. |
| Draws are fair and auditable | The entry list is locked and its fingerprint recorded before the draw. The winner is picked by a published, repeatable method, and every step is written to a tamper-evident log. Published results cannot be changed. |
| Legal first | Paid ticket draws are **off** by default, and paid entry is blocked in VIC and NSW, until Australian legal advice is obtained. |

## Who uses it

| Role | ID format | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer | `BUY-000001` | Buy products, enquire, confirm high-value purchases, claim or buy tickets, request refunds, view draw results |
| Individual seller | `SEL-000001` | List products, manage orders and enquiries, report offline sales, create offers, request campaigns, see commissions and statements |
| Business seller | `BUS-000001` | As above, with ABN/ACN and authorised representative details |
| Super Admin | `ADM-000001` | Approve everything, manage finance, run draws, configure rules, manage content |

IDs are permanent and generated by the server. A buyer who becomes a seller keeps their Buyer ID and also receives a Seller ID.

## Main features

- **Marketplace:** categories and subcategories, with category-specific fields (cars, property, phones, laptops, machinery…) that admins can extend without code; search and filters; featured listings; wishlist.
- **Checkout:** online checkout for everyday products, including delivery fees, offers and coupons. Cars, property and other high-value items are **enquiry only**: the seller reports the sale, the buyer confirms, an admin approves, and commission is invoiced.
- **Commission:** default rates are 5% general, 1% cars, 0.5% property and 3% machinery. Rates can be set per category or per seller. Admins can approve, adjust, waive or reverse commission, always with a reason. Sellers can never change rates or mark invoices paid.
- **Payouts:** online sales pay out to the seller net of commission (Stripe Connect).
- **Promotions:**
  - Campaigns are free or paid, never both.
  - Ticket numbers are unique; maximum tickets per buyer is enforced; the draw runs on a fixed date or when tickets sell out.
  - Draw date extensions need admin approval, and participants are notified.
  - Prize delivery is tracked.
- **Dashboards:** buyer, seller and admin dashboards with notifications, CSV exports and a monthly seller statement.
- **Security:**
  - Passwords are hashed, sessions use httpOnly cookies, and roles are checked on every page and action.
  - Sensitive fields are encrypted, uploads are checked to be the file type they claim, and seller documents are private.
  - Rate limits and bot traps, security headers, and a full audit log.

## Technology

Next.js 15 (React 19, TypeScript), Tailwind CSS v4, Prisma 6 with PostgreSQL, and Docker. Payments go through a provider-independent interface, with a built-in test gateway and a Stripe Connect adapter.
